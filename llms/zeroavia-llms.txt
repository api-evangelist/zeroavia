# ZeroAvia

> ZeroAvia is a UK/US hydrogen-electric aviation company developing hydrogen fuel cell and electric
> propulsion systems for commercial aircraft — the ZA600, ZA2000 and ZA2000 RJ powertrains, an
> electric-aviation component line, and a hydrogen airport refuelling ecosystem. ZeroAvia sells
> certified aerospace hardware to airframers, airlines, lessors and defense customers.
